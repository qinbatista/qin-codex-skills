# Image generation and closure

Apply to every new image, test, draft, retry, edit, transparency repair, and declared image batch. Identify the visual's role, required content, size, aspect ratio, alpha, references, and project style; keep art direction and concrete implementations with the project. Bind the exact request, resource owner, output ID, source identity, and intended synchronization destination before submission.

Use the ChatGPT product webpage through Codex's built-in browser. Do not substitute image tools, direct image APIs, local generation or pixel composition, Chrome/CDP, Ego, or another browser. Use supported authenticated sessions without automating login or copying credentials. If this entry is unavailable, keep the same request pending and report the blocker; do not switch providers or issue a duplicate submission.

1. Submit one owned image job or explicitly declared batch, recording its exact conversation URL and newly generated files and uploaded attachments. Reuse its owned tab while that same job needs an edit; never accumulate generation tabs or open the next job before this one closes.
2. Receive the unchanged native download and compute its SHA. Immediately move it from browser Downloads or temporary staging into the prebound resource owner's exact ID/path, verify the moved bytes, and register native provenance and synchronization intent there. Keep it pending until acceptance; preserve existing originals and immutable versions through the owner's intake contract. Downloads and scratch are transport, not the output owner. Do not repeatedly copy the same download into staging folders, retain duplicate native files, or treat registration as completed synchronization.
3. Inspect the saved image for intended content, reference fidelity, composition, scale, and genuine alpha where required. Preserve native bytes and acceptance criteria; correct content through the same browser entry. Publish only to the authorized exact owner, verify remote bytes/SHA and the consuming reader, then immediately remove this job's scratch and redundant transport copies. Failures keep only minimum recovery inputs/state in the declared recovery owner, with truthful pending or rejected status.
4. After native receipt, necessary QA, and durable local acceptance or rejection, finish account cleanup as a separate state. Delete only this job's exact ChatGPT conversation and independently retained Library files/attachments, then read back both surfaces to confirm the targets are no longer visible. Inspect the actual deletion UI: an exact task-owned Library file's explicitly recoverable move to Trash runs directly under the existing cleanup authorization. Do not apply irreversible-action confirmation to ordinary recoverable Delete; true permanent conversation deletion or Trash purge follows the actual browser's irreversible-action rules. A human reply clearly authorizing the currently identified exact target batch satisfies its action-time confirmation: execute directly without prescribed wording, restatement or another question. Ask only for missing identity or authorization actually required by the browser. Preserve unrelated files, existing user history, and any source still needed by this job. Closing a tab, archiving a conversation, or deleting a local download does not prove account cleanup. Uncertain ownership, remaining dependencies, missing required authorization, or failed UI readback stays `cleanup_required`; finish the local handoff, tab closure, and bounded scratch cleanup below without falsely clearing that account state.

Exact task-owned scratch and request/SHA-verified transport duplicates are already authorized for direct local cleanup. A failure to clean an unrelated directory affects that cleanup only and does not block unrelated authorized work. The next independent image job requires the previous job's verified durable handoff, exact tab closure, and absent scratch; pending account cleanup alone does not hold that job open.

After acceptance or rejection, close this job's owned built-in browser tabs and remove its exact scratch once the required output or minimum recovery state has been handed off and freshly verified. Do this even when account cleanup remains pending; do not keep a browser or temporary files open while waiting for authorization. Preserve the exact request and native SHA, conversation URL, owned file/attachment identities, completed deletion readbacks, and remaining cleanup actions in the project's durable request/evidence store or declared recovery owner. Keep account cleanup, tab closure, and local cleanup as independent truthful states: a closed tab and absent scratch do not mark the account cleaned or the lifecycle fully complete. Once the exact request/native receipts and account inventory are durably replayable, the owned tab has a fresh close readback, and a fresh process confirms the exact scratch is absent, record `closed_with_account_cleanup_pending` and proceed to the next independent image job. Retain `cleanup_required` and its precise recovery targets until actual account readback succeeds; this handoff does not claim acceptance, synchronization, permanent deletion, or cleanup completion. Never close user-opened or unrelated surfaces. Show requested results from their verified output owner. Follow [resource ownership](task-resource-lifecycle.md) for bounded local cleanup.
