# Image generation and closure

Apply to every new image, test, draft, retry, edit, transparency repair, and declared image batch. Identify the visual's role, required content, size, aspect ratio, alpha, references, and project style; keep art direction and concrete implementations with the project. Bind the exact request, resource owner, output ID, source identity, and intended synchronization destination before submission.

Before saving browser evidence or cleanup observations, apply the [external artifact policy](project-cache-artifact-policy.md). Temporary screenshots and JSON receipts go directly into the resolved external task directory. Pending account cleanup retains only exact target identities and necessary state in its declared runtime/recovery owner; it does not retain disposable screenshots or move canonical asset storage.

Use the ChatGPT product webpage through Codex's built-in browser. Do not substitute image tools, direct image APIs, local generation or pixel composition, Chrome/CDP, Ego, or another browser. Use supported authenticated sessions without automating login or copying credentials. If this entry is unavailable, keep the same request pending and report the blocker; do not switch providers or issue a duplicate submission.

1. Submit each owned image job once, recording its exact conversation URL, output owner and newly generated files/attachments. Independent jobs may run in parallel with separate tabs and output IDs; keep one writer per tab and shared account surface. Reuse a job's tab for its edits. While generation runs, prepare downstream inputs, output paths and QA, or run cleanup of unrelated confirmed disposable files. Use Workflow's bounded waits; never resubmit a possibly accepted job because its page is slow.
2. Receive the unchanged native download and compute its SHA. Immediately move it from browser Downloads or temporary staging into the prebound resource owner's exact ID/path, verify the moved bytes, and register native provenance and synchronization intent there. Keep it pending until acceptance; preserve existing originals and immutable versions through the owner's intake contract. Downloads and scratch are transport, not the output owner. Do not repeatedly copy the same download into staging folders, retain duplicate native files, or treat registration as completed synchronization.
3. Inspect the saved image for intended content, reference fidelity, composition, scale, and genuine alpha where required. Preserve native bytes and acceptance criteria; correct content through the same browser entry. Publish only to the authorized exact owner, verify remote bytes/SHA and the consuming reader, then immediately remove this job's scratch and redundant transport copies. Failures keep only minimum recovery inputs/state in the declared recovery owner, with truthful pending or rejected status.
4. After native receipt, QA and durable local acceptance or rejection, submit authorized cleanup for only this job's exact conversation and independently retained Library files/attachments. An authoritative acknowledgement completes the submission step; continue independent work without waiting for a stale UI. Make at most one immediate and one bounded final readback. Record `submitted_verification_pending` when backend absence is unproved; never claim that a click or submission proves deletion. An explicitly recoverable move to Trash runs under the existing cleanup authorization. True permanent conversation deletion or Trash purge follows the actual browser's irreversible-action rules. A human reply clearly authorizing the currently identified exact target batch satisfies its action-time confirmation without prescribed wording or another question. Keep `cleanup_required` as a separate flag for missing identity/authorization or unproved cleanup; a failed readback never erases an authoritative acknowledgement. Preserve unrelated history, files and sources still needed by the job.

Exact task-owned scratch and request/SHA-verified transport duplicates are authorized for direct local cleanup. A blocked account action, stale page or unrelated directory cleanup blocks only its actual dependents. Independent image jobs and other ready work may continue with distinct ownership; do not make account readback or tab closure an artificial gate.

At final cleanup, return to each job's exact owned tab for the bounded last check, then close it when no edit or consumer needs it. Remove scratch after the required output or minimum recovery state is handed off and freshly verified, even if account cleanup is pending. Preserve the request, native SHA, conversation URL, file/attachment identities, authoritative acknowledgements and remaining recovery actions in the declared output/recovery owner. Keep account cleanup, tab closure and local cleanup as separate truthful states. With verified handoff, tab closure and scratch absence, record `closed_with_account_cleanup_pending` if necessary; retain `cleanup_required` and exact recovery targets until account readback succeeds. Do not keep tabs open or repeatedly poll while waiting for authorization. Never close user-opened or unrelated surfaces. Show results from their verified owner and follow [resource ownership](task-resource-lifecycle.md).
